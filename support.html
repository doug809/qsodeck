Common questions

How do I unlock Pro or restore it on a new device?
Open Settings (gear icon on the Logs tab) and tap Unlock QSO Deck Pro or Restore Purchase. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

My logs aren't syncing between devices.
iCloud sync is part of Pro. Sign in to the same Apple ID on both devices, make sure iCloud Drive is on, and open the app on each. After unlocking Pro, close and reopen the app once to start syncing. Settings › Sync status shows what's happening.

Callsign lookups show no name.
The free HamDB service is used by default. For QRZ.com details, choose QRZ.com in Settings and enter your QRZ login; full details need a QRZ XML subscription. Contacts logged offline are looked up when you reconnect.

How do I upload to LoTW?
In TQSL on your computer, choose Callsign Certificates, select your call and save the certificate file (.p12). Send it to your device, then import it in Settings › Logbook of the World and enter its password.

How do I upload to QRZ Logbook?
Copy your API key from logbook.qrz.com under Settings › API and paste it into Settings › QRZ Logbook upload.

Where are my backups?
In the Files app under QSO Deck › Backups. A fresh ADIF copy of the log is written after every contact.

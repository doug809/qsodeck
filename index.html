A logbook for amateur radio on iPhone and iPad. General logs, contests, Parks on the Air and Summits on the Air, with callsign lookups, dupe checking, QRZ and LoTW uploads, and solar and band conditions.

QSO Deck is a free download on the App Store. QSO Deck Pro is a one time purchase that adds uploads, iCloud sync, contest logging, maps and ADIF import.
